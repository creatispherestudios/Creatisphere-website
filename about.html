About Creatisphere

The story behind the solar system.

A creative business consulting practice built in Atlanta, for creatives everywhere.

A Note From the Founder

If you are reading this, you are becoming part of something built because it did not exist yet. Creatisphere started with a simple belief: creatives deserve real support to build a real business, not just likes, not a side hustle that never quite takes off, but an actual creative universe of guidance, tools, and community moving in the same direction.

Like a solar system, each of us brings our own light: our talents, our ideas, our way of seeing the world. But a creative business needs more than talent. It needs strategy, structure, and people around you who want to see you win.

Creatisphere exists to close the gap between talented creatives and the consulting, tools, and community they need to turn what they do into something that lasts. Every decision gets measured against one question. Does this actually help a creative build a real business? If yes, we build it. If not, we skip it.
