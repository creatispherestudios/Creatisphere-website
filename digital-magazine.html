Issue One Feature

Meet this issue's featured creative.

Amara Reed

@amarareedphoto

"Every discipline has its own light. Mine just happens to come through a lens."

Amara is a portrait and editorial photographer based in Atlanta with an eye for warm, natural light. Her editorial series on the city's creative studios is the centerpiece of this issue, capturing the quiet, unglamorous work behind the finished frame.

She joined Creatisphere looking for collaborators outside her usual circle, and found a community that treats every discipline, from music to modeling to design, as equally worth documenting.
